About Bhagavathi Chits

27+ Years of Financial Discipline & Member Trust

Established in 1996/1997 by Ex-Vijaya Bank officer Mr. C. Karunakar Shetty, Bhagavathi Chits Pvt. Ltd. operates government-approved chit groups strictly under the Government of Karnataka Chit Funds Act, 1982.

Today, we proudly serve 1,000+ subscribers across Karnataka and Maharashtra—including third-generation investors, industrialists, builders, hospitality leaders, and professionals—with a monthly turnover nearing ₹7 Crore.

The Bhagavathi Advantage

Why Choose Bhagavathi Chit Funds?

Built on banking-grade governance, zero defaults, and personalized financial care since 1994/1997.

100% FD Lien Security

Before launching any chit group, we place a Fixed Deposit equal to the entire chit value in a Nationalised Bank with lien to the Registrar of Chits.

Repayment Protection

Every prized payout is backed by thorough subscriber screening and adequate security covering future installments to protect non-prized members.

Transparent Monthly Auctions

Auctions run strictly once a month for 5 minutes (5% min to 30% max bid), with equal dividend sharing credited transparently to members.

Member Passbook Facility

Every member receives an official passbook after the second installment for crystal-clear tracking of monthly contributions and dividends.

Up to 18% Dividend Returns

Outperform standard bank deposits (up to 10% p.a.) with steady monthly chit dividends insulated from stock market fluctuations.

Prompt Cheque Payouts

We prioritize timely prize disbursements via verified bank cheques alongside flexible UPI QR and doorstep collection facilities.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on chit safety, dividends, duration, and payments at Bhagavathi Chits Pvt. Ltd.

We are a 27+ year old government-registered chit fund company established in 1996/1997 with an impeccable track record. We have never had a default and have always kept our commitments, operating under regular and periodical scrutiny by the Office of the Registrar of Chits.
Before starting any chit group, we place a Fixed Deposit equal to 100% of the chit denomination with a Nationalised Bank under lien to the Registrar of Chits. We operate via formal banking channels/cheques, and prized money is released only after obtaining adequate securities covering future installments.
Traditional bank deposits typically fetch up to 10% p.a., whereas chit funds can deliver effective dividend returns of up to 18%. Unlike mutual funds that fluctuate with equity market volatility, chit group operations remain unaffected by stock market swings.
We offer flexible registered chit groups starting from ₹2 Lakhs up to ₹1 Crore (including ₹2L, ₹3L, ₹4L, ₹5L, ₹6L, ₹7.5L, ₹10L, ₹15L, ₹20L, ₹25L, ₹50L, and ₹1 Crore schemes).
The duration of our chit groups ranges from 20 months, 25 months, 30 months, up to a maximum of 40 months, where the number of months matches the number of subscribers in that group.

Subscriber KYC Checklist

As a government-registered chit fund under the Chit Funds Act, 1982, subscriber screening keeps every group safe and default-free.

  • Identity Proof: PAN Card & Aadhaar Card copy
  • Address Proof: Recent Utility Bill / Aadhaar / Driving License
  • Photographs: 2 Recent Passport-size photographs
  • Bank Details: Cancelled Cheque / Bank Passbook front page (for cheque payouts)
  • Completed Form: Signed Bhagavathi Chits Application Form

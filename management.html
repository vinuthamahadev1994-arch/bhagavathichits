Founder & Managing Director

Mr. C. Karunakar Shetty

Born on 29th June 1952 at Chantaru, Udupi district, Mr. C. Karunakar Shetty completed his B.Com at Gadag and holds a Post Graduation Degree in Commerce from Karnataka University, along with being a Certified Associate Member of the Indian Institute of Bankers (C.A.IIB).

Appointed by Vijaya Bank in Gadag, he served for over 20 years across the banking industry including the Foreign Exchange Department, and authored several guides and textbooks for B.Com students. In 1996, he founded Bhagavathi Chits Pvt. Ltd., followed by Bhagavathi Stocks in 1999, and serves as a Director at TPS Cold Chain.

"Integrity, strict documentation, and prompt payouts are the foundation on which three generations of families trust Bhagavathi Chits."

Our History

Growing Since 1997 Across Karnataka & Maharashtra

Headquartered in Basaveshwaranagar, Bangalore with branches in Mysore and Udupi, Bhagavathi Chits currently handles nearly ₹7 Crore per month in chit volume, catering to 1,000+ subscribers including industrialists, hospitality sector leaders, builders, fishing industry entrepreneurs, government employees, and professionals.

All chits are registered with the Registrar of Co-operative Societies and conducted strictly in accordance with the Government of Karnataka Chit Fund Act, 1982.

Bhagavathi Chits Pvt. Ltd. is a government-approved registered chit fund company serving Karnataka and Maharashtra since 1997. A two-in-one saving-cum-borrowing financial institution operating strictly under the Chit Funds Act, 1982.
